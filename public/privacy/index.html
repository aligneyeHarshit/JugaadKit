Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version

JugaadKit tools run in your browser. The files you open, such as PDFs and signature images, are processed on your own device and are not sent to our servers.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect, upload, store or view your files.
  • We have no accounts, so we hold no passwords or profiles.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not show ads.

What may be collected

  • Basic visit statistics. We may use privacy-friendly, cookie-less analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count page visits and see which pages are popular. This does not identify you personally.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical request data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Email you send us. If you contact us at hello@jugaadkit.com, we keep your message and address only to reply.

Cookies

JugaadKit does not set tracking or advertising cookies.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. We do not load third-party scripts, fonts or advertising from other sites.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions: hello@jugaadkit.com
