Frequently asked questions

Is JugaadKit really free?

Yes. Every JugaadKit tool is free to use, with no signup, no watermarks and no usage limits.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. JugaadKit tools run in your browser using JavaScript, so your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to our servers. You can check this yourself by opening your browser's developer tools and watching the Network tab while you use a tool.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no accounts, logins or email requirements. Open a tool and use it.

What does "Jugaad" mean?

Jugaad is a Hindi word for a clever, resourceful quick fix: solving a problem with whatever you have at hand. JugaadKit is a kit of small tools that solve everyday jobs the simple way.

Is a signature added with the Sign PDF tool legally valid?

The tool places an image of your signature onto your PDF. It is not a certified digital signature. Many everyday documents accept an image signature, but requirements differ by country, document and recipient, so check with the party who asked you to sign. If you need a certified digital signature, such as a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) or Aadhaar eSign in India, use a certified provider.

Which browsers and devices work?

Any modern browser: current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on desktop, Android and iPhone.

Which tools are coming next?

More PDF and image utilities are planned, such as compress, merge and resize. Every tool will follow the same rules: free, no signup, and processed in your browser. Send suggestions to hello@jugaadkit.com.
