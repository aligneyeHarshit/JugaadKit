How to sign a PDF online

  1. Choose your PDF. Select the file from your device. It opens in your browser.
  2. Add your signature image. Upload a PNG or JPG of your signature. Tick "Remove white background" for a clean, transparent result.
  3. Draw where it goes. Drag a rectangle on the page. The signature fits inside it. Move it or drag a corner dot to resize.
  4. Download. Click "Download signed PDF". Repeat steps 2 and 3 first if you need more signatures or pages.

About image signatures

This tool adds an image of your signature to a PDF. That is different from a certified digital signature, which uses a cryptographic certificate. Image signatures are widely accepted for everyday forms and letters, but some contracts and government processes require a certified signature, so check with the person or organisation asking you to sign.

Sign PDF: frequently asked questions

How do I sign a PDF online for free?

Open the JugaadKit Sign PDF tool, choose your PDF, upload an image of your signature, drag a rectangle where the signature should go, then click Download signed PDF. No signup or software is needed.

Is it safe to sign a PDF here? Is my file uploaded?

Your PDF and signature are processed entirely in your browser and are never uploaded to any server. You can confirm this by watching the Network tab in your browser's developer tools while you use the tool.

What do I need to use my signature?

An image of your signature in PNG or JPG format. A photo of a signature on white paper works: tick "Remove white background" and the tool makes the background transparent for you.

Can I add more than one signature, or sign several pages?

Yes. Upload as many signature images as you like, select one in the list, and draw a box for each place it should appear. You can use different pages, and move or resize any box before downloading.

Is this a legally valid electronic signature?

The tool places an image of your signature onto the PDF. It is not a certified digital signature (such as a DSC or Aadhaar eSign in India). Many everyday documents accept image signatures, but the rules depend on your country, the document and who receives it, so confirm with the recipient. This is not legal advice.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in current mobile browsers on Android and iPhone. You can drag and resize the signature box with your finger.

Can it sign a password-protected or encrypted PDF?

No. Remove the password protection first, then sign the file.

Does it change anything else in my PDF?

No. Your original pages are kept as they are, and the signature image is added on top at the position and size you choose.
